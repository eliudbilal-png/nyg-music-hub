ABOUT US

NYG GROUP SONGEA is a registered Community-Based Organization (CBO No. NA-6913) based in Songea, Tanzania.

The organization works through its ministry, music and community development initiatives, including NYG WORSHIP and NYG MUSIC HUB.

NYG GROUP SONGEA is also registered with the National Arts Council of Tanzania (BASATA) and is licensed to conduct arts activities under Registration No. BST-9684-2026-1645.

The BASATA licence is valid from 10 September 2026 to 10 September 2027.

📄 View BASATA Licence

NYG GROUP SONGEA was founded and is led by Eliud Davies Bilal.

VISION

To build a transformed, inspired and empowered community through faith, music, creativity and community development.

MISSION

To use faith, music, media and community initiatives to inspire, empower and positively transform lives.

KUHUSU SISI

NYG GROUP SONGEA ni Shirika la Kijamii lililosajiliwa (CBO Na. NA-6913) lenye makao yake Songea, Tanzania.

Shirika linaendesha shughuli za huduma, muziki na maendeleo ya jamii, ikiwemo NYG WORSHIP na NYG MUSIC HUB.

NYG GROUP SONGEA pia imesajiliwa na Baraza la Sanaa la Taifa (BASATA) na imepewa kibali cha kuendesha shughuli za sanaa chini ya Namba ya Usajili BST-9684-2026-1645. Kibali cha BASATA ni halali kuanzia 10 Septemba 2026 hadi 10 Septemba 2027.

Matukio ya NYG

MTUME BILALI ATANGAZA MWEZI WA KITUME

Mtumishi wa Mungu na Kiongozi Mkuu wa Huduma ya Nuru Yang’aa Gizani – NYG GRACELAND CENTER, Msamala, Songea, Tanzania, Balozi Apostle Bilali, ametangaza rasmi Oktoba 2026 kuwa Mwezi wa Kitume. Kilele chake kitakuwa tarehe 25/10/2026, ambapo Ibada Kuu ya APOSTOLIC DAY itafanyika.

Katika kikao chake na watenda kazi Jumamosi hii, Apostle Bilali amethibitisha kuwa mwezi huu utakuwa na misheni mbalimbali, zikiwemo kuwatembelea wafungwa na kuwapatia mahitaji muhimu, kuhubiri Injili mitaani na kufanya semina.

Mwezi wa Kitume utahitimishwa kwa Ibada Kuu pamoja na Tamasha la Sifa tarehe 25/10/2026. Waimbaji kutoka NYG WORSHIP watashiriki na kulipamba tamasha hilo.

Apostle Bilali ametoa mwongozo wa namna ya kuenenda katika Mwezi huu wa Kitume na amewakaribisha watu wote kushiriki matukio mbalimbali yatakayofanyika katika mwezi huu.
